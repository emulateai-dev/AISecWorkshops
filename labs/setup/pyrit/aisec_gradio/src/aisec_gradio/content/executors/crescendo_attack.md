# Crescendo attack

**Crescendo**-style attacks progressively escalate or refine prompts across steps (conceptually “turning up” pressure or detail). Implementations in PyRIT coordinate with **chat** targets and memory to track multi-turn progress.

**When to study**

- Understanding **multi-turn** jailbreak dynamics.
- Comparing with single-shot **prompt sending** baselines.

**Workshop use**

Explain the algorithm from docs; live execution belongs in a controlled lab with **stub** runner here unless extended later.
